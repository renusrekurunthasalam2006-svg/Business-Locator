🚀 Problem Statement

Entrepreneurs often start businesses without reliable local market data, leading to oversaturated markets and high failure rates.

Most small business owners rely on assumptions or trends instead of real demand analysis when choosing a business. This results in too many similar businesses in the same area, while other essential services remain unavailable.

⚠️ Key Issue

There is no simple system available to analyze local business density and demand patterns to guide entrepreneurs effectively.

💡 Opportunity

An intelligent opportunity-gap analysis system can identify underserved business sectors in a specific locality and help entrepreneurs make data-driven decisions.
